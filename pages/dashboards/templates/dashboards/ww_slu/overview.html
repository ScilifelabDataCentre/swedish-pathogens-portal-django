The data presented in this dashboard originate from the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU) and the Swedish Environmental Epidemiology Centre (SEEC). The monitoring includes detection and quantification of generic material from influenza A virus, influenza B virus, respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) and SARS CoV-2 in untreated wastewater. We thank all participating wastewater treatment plants for their invaluable efforts in collecting samples and associated data. For more information kindly visit methodology page.
