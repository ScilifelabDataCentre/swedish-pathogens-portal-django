Introduction

The data presented on this page was generated in the SLU (Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences) laboratories of SEEC (Swedish Environmental Epidemiology Center). The project was part of SciLifeLab’s Pandemic Laboratory Preparedness (PLP) Program, led by Anna J. Székely (Department of Aquatic Sciences and Assessment, SLU). Wastewater analyses were overseen by Anna J. Székely and Maja Malmberg (Virology Unit of the Department of Biomedical Science and Veterinary Public Health, SLU). This page describes the historic quantification of SARS-CoV-2 levels in wastewater across multiple Swedish cities. The project achieved the broadest geographic coverage across Sweden, covering data for approximately 43% of the Swedish population.

Please note that the data and visualisations on this page are historic snapshots. The scores in the datasets and plots below are final, although minor corrections may have been applied retrospectively.

Methods

For most cities, raw untreated wastewater samples representative of a single day are collected using flow-compensated samplers at the WWTP. Uppsala is an exception: since week 16 of 2021, daily samples have been combined into a single composite weekly sample before analysis.

Samples collected up to and including week 18 of 2021 were concentrated using the electronegative filtration method (Ahmed et al., 2020). From week 19 of 2021 onwards, viral genomic material has been concentrated and extracted via the direct capture method using the Maxwell RSC Enviro TNA kit (Promega).

Absolute quantification of SARS-CoV-2 copy numbers is performed using One-Step RT-qPCR. Until week 31 of 2023 the CDC N1 assay was used; from week 32 of 2023 the Flu SC2 Multiplex Assay has been applied. The pepper mild mottle virus (PMMoV), quantified via a modified version of the assay from Zhang et al. (2006), serves as an estimator of faecal content (Symonds et al., 2019). Further details and evaluation of PMMoV normalisation in Sweden are available in Isaksson et al. (2022).

The graphs and dataset represent the ratio of Flu SC2 Multiplex (a proxy for SARS-CoV-2 content) to PMMoV (proxy for faecal content), multiplied by 1000. Older data was transformed using conversion factors derived from overlapping periods where both old and new assays ran in parallel, enabling a consistent time series.

Historic wastewater data for Umeå and Örebro

This section combines the previously separate dashboards for Umeå and Örebro. It summarises data recorded between October 2020 and June 2021, after which a new methodology was adopted. The latest data for SLU’s national monitoring programme is available in the sections above.

The measurements were collected as part of a project led by associate professor Maja Malmberg (SLU) in collaboration with the SciLifeLab COVID-19 National Research Program and associate professor Mette Myrmel (Norwegian University of Life Sciences). Samples from the wastewater treatment facilities in Umeå and Örebro were analysed using ultrafiltration and qPCR. Primers verified by Corman et al. (2020) were used to detect SARS-CoV-2, and qPCR results were normalised against PMMoV levels.

Up until January 2021, three wastewater samples were collected per site each week and pooled for a weekly estimate. From January 2021 onwards, sampling occurred weekly. Each plot reports the amount of SARS-CoV-2 per week relative to the amount on 6 November 2020.
